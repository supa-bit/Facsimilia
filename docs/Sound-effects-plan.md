# Sound effects: the plan

For the Ledger question "Which sound effects first" (Sound and music).
Your answers so far: sound effects for clicks, coins, marching, battle,
sea and crowds, varied by era; ambient sound of what's under the camera;
music, effects and ambient with their own volumes.

Each line is one sound. "Now" means the game already has the moment it
plays on; "Later" means it waits on a system not built yet. Lengths are
rough. A take costs about 17 credits (a third of a cent) for a short sound;
at two takes each, the whole first list (about 75 sounds) costs about
2,500 credits (about 45 cents). Looping sounds (ambient) are longer and cost
a little more.

The court (births, marriages, deaths, plots), diplomacy (envoys arriving),
the battle map (orders, charges, arrows, elephants), sieges in detail,
the opening (wind over Göbekli Tepe, a village at Çatalhöyük), and

gunpowder from about 1350, engines in the modern age).
