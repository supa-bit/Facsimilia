# Ornament sources

Every strip is cut from a photograph of the original object (tools/ornament/ornament.py). The museum record is the proof of what it is.

- `assyrian_lotus_bud.png`: Incised furniture plaque with a frieze of lotus blossoms and buds (Assyrian, Mesopotamia, ca. 9th–8th century BCE); Ivory, paint traces. The Metropolitan Museum of Art 67.22.8, https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/325874. Public domain (Met Open Access, CC0).
- `byzantine_marble_interlace.png`: Panel with a Griffin (Byzantine, Greece or the Balkans (possibly), 1250–1300); Marble. The Metropolitan Museum of Art 2000.81, https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/472849. Public domain (Met Open Access, CC0). Sits on the top edge.
- `chinese_shang_dragons.png`: Rectangular cauldron (fangding) (China, 12th–11th century BCE); Bronze. The Metropolitan Museum of Art 49.135.2, https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/60594. Public domain (Met Open Access, CC0). Sits on the top edge.
- `egyptian_kheker_meketre.png`: Relief fragment, tomb of Meketre (Middle Kingdom, Egypt, Upper Egypt, Thebes, MMA excavations, 1920, ca. 1981–1975 B.C.); Limestone, paint. The Metropolitan Museum of Art 20.3.974, https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/562132. Public domain (Met Open Access, CC0). Sits on the top edge.
- `french_hours_border_rouen.png`: Leaf from a Book of Hours: Text with Illustrated Border (verso) (France, Rouen, c. 1510); ink, tempera, and liquid gold on vellum. The Cleveland Museum of Art 2001.79.b, https://clevelandart.org/art/2001.79.b. CC0 (Cleveland Museum of Art Open Access). Sits on the side edge.
- `greek_geometric_meander.png`: Terracotta krater (Greek, Attic, ca. 750–735 BCE); Terracotta. The Metropolitan Museum of Art 14.130.14, https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/248904. Public domain (Met Open Access, CC0).
- `inca_diamond_band.png`: Tunic with Diamond Band (Inca, Peru, 1460–1540); Camelid hair, cotton. The Metropolitan Museum of Art 1982.365, https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/314528. Public domain (Met Open Access, CC0).
- `indian_burhanpur_flower_band.png`: Panel of a Tent Lining (India, India, Deccan, Burhanpur, 1700–1740); Cotton; plain weave, mordant painted and dyed, resist dyed. The Metropolitan Museum of Art 2005.251, https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/455064. Public domain (Met Open Access, CC0). Sits on the bottom edge.
- `ottoman_iznik_saz_border.png`: Tile Panel (Turkey, Turkey, Iznik, second half 16th century); Stonepaste; polychrome painted under transparent glaze.. The Metropolitan Museum of Art 17.190.2083, https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/447013. Public domain (Met Open Access, CC0). Sits on the top edge.
- `persian_persepolis_rosettes.png`: Relief: figure in a procession (Achaemenid, Iran, ca. 405–359 BCE); Limestone. The Metropolitan Museum of Art 39.133, https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/323723. Public domain (Met Open Access, CC0). Sits on the top edge.
- `roman_mosaic_cubes.png`: Mosaic floor panel (Roman, 2nd century CE); Stone, tile, and glass. The Metropolitan Museum of Art 38.11.12, https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/253565. Public domain (Met Open Access, CC0). Sits on the top edge.
