# Ornament sources

Every strip is cut from a photograph of the original object (tools/ornament/ornament.py). The museum record is the proof of what it is.

- `assyrian_lotus_bud.png`: Incised furniture plaque with a frieze of lotus blossoms and buds (Assyrian, Mesopotamia, ca. 9th–8th century BCE); Ivory, paint traces. The Metropolitan Museum of Art 67.22.8, https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/325874. Public domain (Met Open Access, CC0).
- `egyptian_kheker_meketre.png`: Relief fragment, tomb of Meketre (Middle Kingdom, Egypt, Upper Egypt, Thebes, MMA excavations, 1920, ca. 1981–1975 B.C.); Limestone, paint. The Metropolitan Museum of Art 20.3.974, https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/562132. Public domain (Met Open Access, CC0). Sits on the top edge.
- `greek_geometric_meander.png`: Terracotta krater (Greek, Attic, ca. 750–735 BCE); Terracotta. The Metropolitan Museum of Art 14.130.14, https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/248904. Public domain (Met Open Access, CC0).
